About

About Pranaura

Real-estate networking and introduction platform.

Pranaura acts as a professional intermediary and networking platform connecting builders, developers, landowners and prospective investors or buyers in Mysore and Karnataka.


We do not buy, sell or guarantee projects. Instead, we facilitate introductions and business discussions between interested parties based on shared requirements.


Every interaction is guided by professionalism, clear communication and a commitment to fostering valuable business connections.
